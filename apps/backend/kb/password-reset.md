---
title: How to reset your password
visibility: PUBLIC
published: true
category: General
---

Use Forgot password on the login screen. Enter the account email, open the reset link within 30 minutes, and set a password with at least 8 characters.

If no email arrives, check spam, wait two minutes, and try once more. After five failed sign-in attempts the account locks for 15 minutes. Nova can walk through this; a human is only needed if the mailbox never receives the link.
